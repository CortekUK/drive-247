Reviewed5. MileagePortal, booking site

Sets the included miles for each hire length and the charge for going over. Offers the paid unlimited mileage upgrade and sets its prices. Confirms the booking site shows the right mileage and upgrade price.

Reviewed6. Extras and surchargesPortal, booking site

Money: tips for Haris

Use these whenever a price or total appears during the checks.

  1. Do the sum yourself first, on a calculator, before you look at the app's number.
  2. Check each line, not just the total: rate, days, extras, surcharge, discount, tax, fee and deposit.
  3. Use odd numbers like 47.35, so rounding mistakes show up. Round numbers hide them.
  4. Compare the same price everywhere it appears: the car's page in the portal and the booking page.
  5. Change one thing at a time, so you can tell what moved the price.
  6. Try the edges: 6 and 7 days, the first monthly day, a weekend, a holiday.
  7. Wrong by even one cent counts. Stop and tell Ghulam straight away.
  8. Never fix a price calculation alone, because it can change prices for live tenants.
