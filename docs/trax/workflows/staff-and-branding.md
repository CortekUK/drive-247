# Audit logs, Branding and appearance, Staff administration

## Purpose

Prepared operator guidance for TRAX, verified against the portal source files listed below. Guidance describes existing screens only; TRAX performs none of these actions.
<!-- trax:staff_add:en -->
Only a head admin can add staff. Open **Settings** with the gear beside your organization name, or **Organization settings** in its menu, then select **Team** under **Business** (only head admins see it). On **Manage Users**, select **Add User**. In **Add New User**, enter **Full Name**, **Email Address** and **Role**: **Admin** (full access except user management), **Manager** (custom tab access), **Operations** (day-to-day operations) or **Viewer** (read-only). Head Admin cannot be assigned here. A Manager needs at least one tab ticked under **Tab Permissions**. Select **Create User**. The account gets a temporary password and a welcome email with login details is sent. **User Created Successfully** shows the **Temporary Password** only once; copy it before selecting **I've Saved the Credentials**, because it cannot be retrieved later. If the welcome email fails, the notice says so and you must give the password to the person yourself. The new user is asked to change the password after signing in and shows a **Temp Password** badge. An email already used on this account is rejected. TRAX cannot create staff accounts, see passwords or send the welcome email.
<!-- /trax -->

Welcome email na ja sake to notice bata dega; phir password khud dein. Naye user ko sign in ke baad password badalne ka kaha jata hai aur **Temp Password** badge dikhta hai. <!-- trax:staff_roles:en -->
Only a head admin can change roles and manager permissions. Open **Settings**, select **Team** under **Business**, then open the three-dot **Actions** menu on the person's row. **Change Role** opens **Change User Role**: choose a **New Role** and select **Update Role**. **Admin** has full access except user management, **Manager** gets only the tabs you grant, **Operations** covers day-to-day work and **Viewer** is read-only. Head Admin rows have no **Change Role** option, and nobody can change their own role. For a Manager, tick tabs under **Tab Permissions** and switch each between **View** and **Edit**; new ticks start as View, and tabs marked **View only** cannot be edited. Settings sub-tabs such as **General**, **Branding** and **Templates** appear only after **Settings** is ticked, and a manager needs both. At least one tab is required. For an existing manager use **Edit Permissions**, then **Save Permissions**. Managers never see **Team** and can never open **Manage Users**. TRAX cannot change roles or permissions.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:staff_roles:ur-Latn -->
Roles aur manager permissions sirf head admin badal sakta hai. **Settings** kholein, **Business** ke neeche **Team** chunein, phir us shakhs ki row ka teen-dot **Actions** menu kholein. **Change Role** se **Change User Role** khulta hai: **New Role** chunein aur **Update Role** dabayein. **Admin** ko user management ke ilawa full access hai, **Manager** ko sirf diye gaye tabs, **Operations** rozana ka kaam karta hai aur **Viewer** sirf dekh sakta hai. Head Admin row mein **Change Role** nahi hota, aur koi apna role khud nahi badal sakta. Manager ke liye **Tab Permissions** mein tabs tick karein aur har ek ko **View** ya **Edit** par rakhein; naya tick View se shuru hota hai, aur **View only** tabs edit nahi ho sakte. **General**, **Branding** ya **Templates** jaise Settings sub-tabs tabhi dikhte hain jab **Settings** tick ho, aur manager ko dono chahiye. Kam az kam ek tab zaroori hai. Maujooda manager ke liye **Edit Permissions**, phir **Save Permissions**. Manager ko **Team** nahi dikhta aur woh **Manage Users** kabhi nahi khol sakta. TRAX roles ya permissions nahi badalta.

<!-- trax:staff_deactivate:en -->
Only a head admin can deactivate staff. Open **Settings**, select **Team** under **Business**, open the three-dot **Actions** menu on the person's row and choose **Deactivate**. It applies immediately, with no confirmation step, and the status changes to **Inactive**. An inactive account cannot sign in, and the person's existing sessions are signed out. You cannot deactivate yourself. The person stays in the list; choose **Activate** on the same row to restore access. To issue a new password instead, choose **Reset Password**, use **Generate** or type one under **New Password**, copy it, then select **Reset Password**. A **Can't sign in** badge means the row has no login account. Activation and deactivation are recorded in **Audit Logs**. TRAX cannot deactivate, activate or reset passwords for staff.
<!-- /trax -->

Activate aur deactivate **Audit Logs** mein record hote hain. TRAX staff ko deactivate, activate ya password reset nahi karta.

<!-- trax:settings_branding:en -->
Open **Settings** with the gear beside your organization name, or **Organization settings** in its menu, then select **Branding** under **Business**. Managers need the Settings and Branding grants; without edit rights the tab shows a view-only notice and no save buttons. In **Brand Identity**, **Application Name** is the name shown in the sidebar and browser title. **Company Logo** takes an image under 5MB and saves as soon as you select **Upload Logo**. The favicon must be under 1MB. **Default Theme Colors** holds **Primary Color**, **Secondary Color** and **Accent Color**; **Light Theme Colors** and **Dark Theme Colors** override them, and **Sync dark theme from light** keeps dark in step. Booking site buttons follow Accent; **Header & Footer Colors** set the booking site header and footer. Select **Save Changes**, or **Revert changes** to drop unsaved edits. **Reset All to Defaults** restores default branding. **Open Appearance** styles the portal itself. Phone, email and address are not on this tab; they are in **Site Settings** in the Website section. TRAX does not change branding.
<!-- /trax -->

<!-- trax:portal_appearance:en -->
**Appearance** styles the portal your team uses. Open **Settings**, **Branding**, then **Open Appearance**. Under **Brand colour**, pick a swatch or **Custom colour**; the portal previews it straight away, and **Fix it for me** appears when text would be hard to read. Under **Logo & name**, set **Portal name** (browser tab and beside your logo), and upload a **Logo** under 5MB and a **Favicon** under 1MB. If warned, **Remove the box** removes a solid logo background and **Create a light version** makes a dark-mode logo. Nothing is kept until you select **Save changes**; **Discard** drops the preview. **Reset** then **Reset colours** restores the default colours and leaves logo, favicon and name unchanged. Saving updates the portal for everyone on your team. Editing needs Branding edit rights; otherwise the page is view-only. Your customers' booking site is styled separately. TRAX does not change appearance.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:portal_appearance:ur-Latn -->
**Appearance** aap ki team ke portal ki look set karta hai. **Settings**, **Branding**, phir **Open Appearance** kholein. **Brand colour** mein swatch ya **Custom colour** chunein; portal foran preview dikhata hai, aur text parhna mushkil ho to **Fix it for me** aata hai. **Logo & name** mein **Portal name** (browser tab aur logo ke saath) likhein, 5MB se chhota **Logo** aur 1MB se chhota **Favicon** upload karein. Warning aaye to **Remove the box** logo ka solid background hatata hai aur **Create a light version** dark mode ke liye logo banata hai. **Save changes** dabaye baghair kuch save nahi hota; **Discard** preview hata deta hai. **Reset** phir **Reset colours** default rang wapas lata hai; logo, favicon aur naam wahi rehte hain. Save karne se poori team ke liye portal badal jata hai. Edit ke liye Branding edit ki ijazat chahiye, warna page view-only hai. Customers ki booking site alag se style hoti hai. TRAX appearance nahi badalta.

<!-- trax:audit_logs:en -->
Select your organization name at the top of the sidebar and choose **Audit Logs**. Managers see it only with the **Audit Logs** grant, which is view only. Each row shows **Date & Time**, **Action**, **Entity**, **Details** and **Performed By**; hover over the details to see everything recorded, and **System** means no staff member is linked. Filter by entity type, action, **Performed by** and a **From date** to **To date** range; **Clear** removes the filters. Entries show 25 per page with **Previous** and **Next**, and at most the 500 most recent matching entries are loaded. **Export CSV** downloads those loaded entries. The **Performed by** list offers only active staff. Actions ending in **View** or **Warn** record that a form or warning was shown, not that a change was saved. Entries cannot be edited or deleted on this page. TRAX cannot read or search audit entries.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:audit_logs:ur-Latn -->
Sidebar ke upar organization ka naam select karein aur **Audit Logs** chunein. Manager ko ye sirf **Audit Logs** grant ke saath dikhta hai, jo view only hai. Har row mein **Date & Time**, **Action**, **Entity**, **Details** aur **Performed By** hai; poori recorded detail ke liye details par hover karein, aur **System** ka matlab hai koi staff member linked nahi. Entity type, action, **Performed by** aur **From date** se **To date** tak filter karein; **Clear** filters hata deta hai. Ek page par 25 entries aati hain, **Previous** aur **Next** se page badlein, aur zyada se zyada 500 taaza matching entries load hoti hain. **Export CSV** yehi loaded entries download karta hai. **Performed by** list mein sirf active staff hain. **View** ya **Warn** par khatam hone wale actions sirf form ya warning dikhne ka record hain, change save hone ka nahi. Is page par entries edit ya delete nahi hotin. TRAX audit entries parh ya search nahi sakta.
