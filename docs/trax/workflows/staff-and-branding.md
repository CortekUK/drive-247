# Audit logs, Branding and appearance, Staff administration

## Purpose

Prepared operator guidance for TRAX, verified against the portal source files listed below. Guidance describes existing screens only; TRAX performs none of these actions.
<!-- trax:staff_add:en -->
Only a head admin can add staff. Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (on the updated portal, the gear beside your name), then select **Team** under **Business** (only head admins see it). On **Manage Users**, select **Add User**. In **Add New User**, enter **Full Name**, **Email Address** and **Role**: **Admin** (full access except user management), **Manager** (custom tab access), **Operations** (day-to-day operations) or **Viewer** (read-only). Head Admin cannot be assigned here. A Manager needs at least one tab ticked under **Tab Permissions**. Select **Create User**. The account gets a temporary password and a welcome email with login details is sent. **User Created Successfully** shows the **Temporary Password** only once; copy it before selecting **I've Saved the Credentials**, because it cannot be retrieved later. If the welcome email fails, the notice says so and you must give the password to the person yourself. The new user is asked to change the password after signing in and shows a **Temp Password** badge. An email already used on this account is rejected. TRAX cannot create staff accounts, see passwords or send the welcome email.
<!-- /trax -->

<!-- trax:staff_roles:en -->
Only a head admin can change roles and manager permissions. Open **Settings**, select **Team** under **Business**, then open the three-dot **Actions** menu on the person's row. **Change Role** opens **Change User Role**: choose a **New Role** and select **Update Role**. **Admin** has full access except user management, **Manager** gets only the tabs you grant, **Operations** covers day-to-day work and **Viewer** is read-only. Head Admin rows have no **Change Role** option, and nobody can change their own role. For a Manager, tick tabs under **Tab Permissions** and switch each between **View** and **Edit**; new ticks start as View, and tabs marked **View only** cannot be edited. Settings sub-tabs such as **General**, **Branding** and **Templates** appear only after **Settings** is ticked, and a manager needs both. At least one tab is required. For an existing manager use **Edit Permissions**, then **Save Permissions**. Managers never see **Team** and can never open **Manage Users**. TRAX cannot change roles or permissions.
<!-- /trax -->

Head Admin row mein **Change Role** nahi hota, aur koi apna role khud nahi badal sakta. Manager ke liye **Tab Permissions** mein tabs tick karein aur har ek ko **View** ya **Edit** par rakhein; naya tick View se shuru hota hai, aur **View only** tabs edit nahi ho sakte. **General**, **Branding** ya **Templates** jaise Settings sub-tabs tabhi dikhte hain jab **Settings** tick ho, aur manager ko dono chahiye. Kam az kam ek tab zaroori hai. Maujooda manager ke liye **Edit Permissions**, phir **Save Permissions**. Manager ko **Team** nahi dikhta aur woh **Manage Users** kabhi nahi khol sakta. TRAX roles ya permissions nahi badalta.

<!-- trax:staff_deactivate:en -->
Only a head admin can deactivate staff. Open **Settings**, select **Team** under **Business**, open the three-dot **Actions** menu on the person's row and choose **Deactivate**. It applies immediately, with no confirmation step, and the status changes to **Inactive**. An inactive account cannot sign in, and the person's existing sessions are signed out. You cannot deactivate yourself. The person stays in the list; choose **Activate** on the same row to restore access. To issue a new password instead, choose **Reset Password**, use **Generate** or type one under **New Password**, copy it, then select **Reset Password**. A **Can't sign in** badge means the row has no login account. Activation and deactivation are recorded in **Audit Logs**. TRAX cannot deactivate, activate or reset passwords for staff.

<!-- trax:settings_branding:en -->
Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (on the updated portal, the gear beside your name), then select **Branding** under **Business**. Managers need the Settings and Branding grants; without edit rights the tab shows a view-only notice and no save buttons. In **Brand Identity**, **Application Name** is the name shown in the sidebar and browser title. **Company Logo** takes an image under 5MB and saves as soon as you select **Upload Logo**. The favicon must be under 1MB. **Default Theme Colors** holds **Primary Color**, **Secondary Color** and **Accent Color**; **Light Theme Colors** and **Dark Theme Colors** override them, and **Sync dark theme from light** keeps dark in step. Booking site buttons follow Accent; **Header & Footer Colors** set the booking site header and footer. Select **Save Changes**, or **Revert changes** to drop unsaved edits. **Reset All to Defaults** restores default branding. **Open Appearance** styles the portal itself. Phone, email and address are not on this tab; they are in **Site Settings** in the Website section. TRAX does not change branding.

<!-- trax:portal_appearance:en -->
**Appearance** styles the portal your team uses. On the updated portal, open **Settings** and select **Branding**; a portal not yet updated shows an older **Branding** tab with **Open Appearance**, laid out differently. **Portal name** is what shows at the top of the sidebar and in the browser tab, with small pictures of both beside the field that follow your typing, and a note when the name is too long for the sidebar and will be cut off. Under **Brand colour**, pick a swatch or type a colour code; the portal repaints around you straight away, and **Fix it for me** appears when text on that colour would be hard to read. **Logos** has two slots, and they are independent. **Square icon** shows in the browser tab and at the top of your sidebar: PNG, WebP, JPG, SVG or ICO, up to 10 MB, square and 128 to 4096 px a side, and **Fit into a square** is offered when an otherwise good image is not square. **Full logo** shows on your sign-in page and your booking website: PNG, WebP, JPG or SVG, up to 10 MB, 400 to 6000 px wide and 100 to 3000 px tall. Best results are a PNG with a transparent background. Each card shows your image where it will really appear, and **Upload**, **Replace** and **Remove** work on that card only. The full logo is never used as the square icon: with no square icon, the tab and the sidebar badge show your portal name's initials on a tile in your brand colour. Nothing is kept until you select **Save changes**; **Reset** puts the saved branding back. Saving updates the portal for everyone on your team. Editing needs Branding edit rights; otherwise the page is view-only. Your customers' booking site is styled separately. TRAX does not change appearance.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:portal_appearance:ur-Latn -->
**Appearance** aap ki team ke portal ki look set karta hai. Naye portal par **Settings** kholein aur **Branding** chunein; jo portal abhi update nahi hua, us mein purana **Branding** tab aur **Open Appearance** hota hai, jiska layout alag hai. **Portal name** wo naam hai jo sidebar ke upar aur browser tab mein dikhta hai; field ke saath dono ki chhoti tasveerein aap ke likhne ke saath badalti hain, aur naam sidebar ke liye lamba ho to note aa jata hai ke wo kat jayega. **Brand colour** mein swatch chunein ya colour code likhein; portal foran us rang mein badal jata hai, aur us rang par text parhna mushkil ho to **Fix it for me** aata hai. **Logos** mein do slot hain aur dono alag alag hain. **Square icon** browser tab aur sidebar ke upar dikhta hai: PNG, WebP, JPG, SVG ya ICO, 10 MB tak, square aur har taraf 128 se 4096 px; tasveer theek ho magar square na ho to **Fit into a square** milta hai. **Full logo** aap ke sign-in page aur booking website par dikhta hai: PNG, WebP, JPG ya SVG, 10 MB tak, 400 se 6000 px chaura aur 100 se 3000 px lamba. Behtareen nateeja transparent background wali PNG se milta hai. Har card aap ki tasveer wahin dikhata hai jahan wo asal mein aayegi, aur **Upload**, **Replace** aur **Remove** sirf usi card par chalte hain. Full logo kabhi square icon ki jagah nahi lagta: square icon na ho to tab aur sidebar badge mein aap ke portal name ke initials aap ke brand rang ki tile par dikhte hain. **Save changes** dabaye baghair kuch save nahi hota; **Reset** save shuda branding wapas laata hai. Save karne se poori team ke liye portal badal jata hai. Edit ke liye Branding edit ki ijazat chahiye, warna page view-only hai. Customers ki booking site alag se style hoti hai. TRAX appearance nahi badalta.

<!-- trax:audit_logs:en -->
On the updated portal, open **Settings** with the gear beside your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then select **Audit Logs** under **Business**. Managers see it only with the **Audit Logs** grant, which is view only. Each row shows **Date & Time**, **Action**, **Entity**, **Details** and **Performed By**; hover over the details to see everything recorded, and **System** means no staff member is linked. On the updated portal a graph above the list counts the loaded entries over the period you pick, with **Views & warnings** as a second line, and the search box at the top of the screen searches the entries already loaded — the action, the entity type, the customer, who performed it and the details, but not the date. The sliders button beside that box turns the graph over to show the filters and carries a number for how many are on. Filter by entity type, action, **Performed by** and a **From date** to **To date** range; **Reset** clears the filters and the search box. Entries load 25 at a time as you scroll, with **Show more** at the foot of the list, and at most the 1,000 most recent matching entries are loaded; when that many come back, the note under the graph names the oldest entry loaded, and days before it are not counted there. A portal not yet updated shows the same five filters in a row under the title with a **Clear** button, no search box and no graph, 25 entries per page with **Previous** and **Next**, and at most the 500 most recent matching entries loaded. **Export CSV** downloads the entries the list is showing. The **Performed by** list offers only active staff. Actions ending in **View** or **Warn** record that a form or warning was shown, not that a change was saved. Entries cannot be edited or deleted on this page. TRAX cannot read or search audit entries.

<!-- trax:audit_logs:ur-Latn -->
Naye portal par sidebar mein sab se neeche apne naam ke saath wale gear se **Settings** kholein, phir **Business** ke neeche **Audit Logs** chunein. Manager ko ye sirf **Audit Logs** grant ke saath dikhta hai, jo view only hai. Har row mein **Date & Time**, **Action**, **Entity**, **Details** aur **Performed By** hai; poori recorded detail ke liye details par hover karein, aur **System** ka matlab hai koi staff member linked nahi. Naye portal par list ke upar ek graph chuni hui muddat ki load shuda entries ginta hai, aur **Views & warnings** doosri line hai; screen ke upar wala search box sirf pehle se load shuda entries mein dhoondta hai — action, entity type, customer, kis ne kiya aur details, magar date nahi. Us box ke saath wala sliders button graph ko palat kar filters dikhata hai aur us par chalu filters ka number aata hai. Entity type, action, **Performed by** aur **From date** se **To date** tak filter karein; **Reset** filters aur search box dono saaf kar deta hai. Scroll karne par entries 25-25 kar ke aati hain, list ke neeche **Show more** hota hai, aur zyada se zyada 1,000 taaza matching entries load hoti hain; itni aa jayein to graph ke neeche wala note sab se purani load shuda entry ka din batata hai, aur us se pehle ke din wahan nahi ginte. Jo portal abhi update nahi hua, us mein yehi paanch filters title ke neeche ek qatar mein hote hain, saath **Clear** button, na search box na graph, ek page par 25 entries **Previous** aur **Next** ke saath, aur zyada se zyada 500 taaza matching entries load hoti hain. **Export CSV** wohi entries download karta hai jo list dikha rahi hai. **Performed by** list mein sirf active staff hain. **View** ya **Warn** par khatam hone wale actions sirf form ya warning dikhne ka record hain, change save hone ka nahi. Is page par entries edit ya delete nahi hotin. TRAX audit entries parh ya search nahi sakta.
