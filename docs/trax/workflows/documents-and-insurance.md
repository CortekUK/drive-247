# Agreements, Insurance, Plates

## Purpose

Prepared operator guidance for TRAX, verified against the portal source files listed below. Guidance describes existing screens only; TRAX performs none of these actions.
<!-- trax:agreements:en -->
**Agreements** in the sidebar lists every agreement with **Customer**, **Email**, **Sent** and **Status** (**Signed**, **Pending signature** or **Failed**). Filter by customer, sent date, status or kind (rental or individual), or search by customer, email or title. A row's menu offers **View**, **Download** once it is signed, and **Resend**, which sends a fresh copy as a new row. To send one rental's agreement, use that rental's **Agreement** stage. **Send agreement** sends one that is not tied to a rental: enter the recipient's name and email, any CC addresses, a title and an optional message, choose a template or **Create new**, then preview it; **Edit** there changes only this agreement, never the template. The **Templates** section on the same page lists every template and starts a new one with **Create your template**. The **Default** template is the one sent from rentals; **Set as default** changes which one that is. The template editor shows the agreement beside a live preview. Its side panel holds the variables (hover one for what it means and an example), the **Signature**, **Initials** and **Date signed** fields to drag into place, and **Your signature** to draw or upload and then **Save & use**. Managers can view agreements but not send them, and editing templates needs the Templates settings grant. Sending uses e-sign credits, unless e-signing is included with your plan. TRAX does not send, sign, resend or void agreements.

<!-- trax:insurances:en -->
To add cover to one rental, use that rental's **Insurance** stage. The Insurances page is not in the sidebar. Its **Policies** tab lists Bonzah policies and uploaded insurance documents with **Policy Status** and **Payment**; search by document or customer name, or toggle **Bonzah Only**. **Generate Insurance** opens **Generate Bonzah Insurance**: pick a rental, choose coverage, then **Purchase Insurance**, which creates the policy and adds an insurance charge to the rental; collect payment afterwards. It is disabled under a **New insurance policies are turned off** banner when Bonzah cannot sell; existing policies stay viewable. On Bonzah rows, **Add Payment** shows while the charge is **Unpaid** or **Partial**, **PDF** downloads the policy and **View Rental** opens the rental. For a customer's own policy, open **AI Verifications**, select **Verify Insurance**, add PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files, then upload and verify; use **Attach** to link it to an active or pending rental. Managers need the Insurances grant. TRAX does not buy, verify or attach cover, take payments, or confirm a vehicle is insured.
<!-- /trax -->

Is ke **Policies** tab mein Bonzah policies aur upload shuda insurance documents **Policy Status** aur **Payment** ke saath nazar aate hain; document ya customer ke naam se search karein, ya **Bonzah Only** lagayein. **Generate Insurance** se **Generate Bonzah Insurance** khulta hai: rental chunein, coverage select karein, phir **Purchase Insurance** dabayein; is se policy banti hai aur rental par insurance charge lag jata hai, payment baad mein lein. Customer ki apni policy ke liye **AI Verifications** kholein, **Verify Insurance** chunein, PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP ya HEIC files add karke upload aur verify karein; phir **Attach** se active ya pending rental se jorein. Managers ko Insurances grant chahiye. TRAX insurance khareedta, verify ya attach nahi karta, payment nahi leta, aur gaari insured hone ki tasdeeq nahi karta.

<!-- trax:plates:en -->
**Plates Management** is not in the sidebar; reach it from search. Select **Add Plate** to open **Add New Plate**. **Plate Number** and **Status** (**Ordered**, **Received**, **Assigned** or **Expired**) are required; the number is saved in capitals without spaces and must be unique. **Vehicle** becomes required when status is **Assigned**, and **Order Date** cannot be in the future for **Received** or **Assigned** plates. **Supplier**, cost, **Retention Document Reference**, **Notes** and a document chosen with **Choose File** (PDF, image or Word file, max 10MB) are optional. Select **Add Plate** to save; linking a vehicle also records the change in that vehicle's history. Filter by status or by **Has Document** / **No Document**, use the search box, and **Export CSV** to download the filtered list. Click a plate number to copy it. In a row's menu, **Edit** reopens the form (save with **Update Plate**) and **View History** shows the plate's timeline. TRAX does not create, edit or delete plates.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:plates:ur-Latn -->
**Plates Management** sidebar mein nahi hai; search se kholein. **Add Plate** dabayein, **Add New Plate** khulega. **Plate Number** aur **Status** (**Ordered**, **Received**, **Assigned** ya **Expired**) zaroori hain; number capital letters mein baghair spaces save hota hai aur unique hona chahiye. Status **Assigned** ho to **Vehicle** zaroori hai, aur **Received** ya **Assigned** plate ki **Order Date** aane wali tareekh nahi ho sakti. **Supplier**, cost, **Retention Document Reference**, **Notes** aur **Choose File** se document (PDF, image ya Word file, zyada se zyada 10MB) optional hain. Save ke liye **Add Plate** dabayein; gaari link karne par us gaari ki history mein bhi entry banti hai. Status ya **Has Document** / **No Document** se filter karein, search box use karein, aur filtered list ke liye **Export CSV** dabayein. Plate number par click karne se copy ho jata hai. Row menu mein **Edit** form dobara kholta hai (**Update Plate** se save) aur **View History** plate ki timeline dikhata hai. TRAX plates banata, edit ya delete nahi karta.

<!-- trax:plate_assign:en -->
In **Plates Management**, open a plate's row menu. **Assign** appears when the plate has no vehicle and opens **Assign Plate to Vehicle**; **Select Vehicle** is required and lists vehicles with Available status. If that vehicle already has a plate, **Plate Replacement Required** warns that its current plate will be set to **Received**; confirm with **Replace & Assign**, otherwise **Assign Plate**. **Assignment Note (Optional)** is added to the vehicle's history. Assigning sets the plate to **Assigned**. **Unassign** removes the vehicle and sets the plate to **Received**. **Mark Expired** appears on any plate that is not already expired. **Delete** is disabled for assigned plates, so unassign first; on an unassigned plate it deletes straight away with no confirmation. **View History** shows the plate's timeline. TRAX does not assign, unassign, expire or delete plates.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:plate_assign:ur-Latn -->
**Plates Management** mein plate ka row menu kholein. Jab plate ke saath koi gaari na ho to **Assign** dikhta hai aur **Assign Plate to Vehicle** kholta hai; **Select Vehicle** zaroori hai aur is mein Available status wali gaariyan aati hain. Agar us gaari par pehle se plate lagi ho to **Plate Replacement Required** batata hai ke purani plate **Received** ho jayegi; **Replace & Assign** se confirm karein, warna **Assign Plate** dabayein. **Assignment Note (Optional)** gaari ki history mein likha jata hai. Assign karne se plate **Assigned** ho jati hai. **Unassign** gaari hata kar plate ko **Received** kar deta hai. **Mark Expired** har us plate par dikhta hai jo pehle se expired na ho. Assigned plate par **Delete** disabled hai, pehle unassign karein; unassigned plate par yeh foran, baghair confirmation, delete kar deta hai. **View History** plate ki timeline dikhata hai. TRAX plates assign, unassign, expire ya delete nahi karta.
