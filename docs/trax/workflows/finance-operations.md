# Credits, Expenses, Fines, Invoices

## Purpose

Prepared operator guidance for TRAX, verified against the portal source files listed below. Guidance describes existing screens only; TRAX performs none of these actions.
<!-- trax:fines_record:en -->
Open **Fines** and choose **Add Fine**; it is shown only to staff with fines edit permission. You can also use **Add a charge** in a rental's **Payments** stage, which preselects that customer and rental, or **Add a fine** in the customer record's **Fines** section. The **Add New Fine** dialog requires **Fine Type** (**Parking Citation**, **Speeding Violation**, or **Other** with a custom name), **Customer**, **Rental**, **Issue Date**, **Due Date** and **Amount**. Choosing the rental sets the vehicle and pre-fills **Reference Number** with the plate; replace it with the ticket reference if you have one. **Issue Date** cannot be in the future; **Due Date** defaults to 28 days later and cannot be earlier. **Notes** and **Evidence Files** (up to 10 files, 20MB each) are optional. **Create Fine** stays disabled until the form is valid. The fine is saved as **Open** and a Fine charge is added to that rental. TRAX does not create, charge, waive or collect fines.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:fines_record:ur-Latn -->
**Fines** kholein aur **Add Fine** chunein; ye sirf fines edit permission wale staff ko dikhta hai. Rental ke **Payments** stage mein **Add a charge** se bhi fine add ho sakta hai (customer aur rental pehle se select hote hain), ya customer record ke **Fines** section mein **Add a fine** se. **Add New Fine** dialog mein **Fine Type** (**Parking Citation**, **Speeding Violation**, ya **Other** ke saath custom naam), **Customer**, **Rental**, **Issue Date**, **Due Date** aur **Amount** zaroori hain. Rental chunne par vehicle khud set ho jati hai aur **Reference Number** mein plate aa jati hai; challan ka reference ho to woh likh dein. **Issue Date** future ki nahi ho sakti; **Due Date** khud 28 din baad set hoti hai aur issue date se pehle nahi ho sakti. **Notes** aur **Evidence Files** (10 files tak, har file 20MB tak) optional hain. Form theek bharne tak **Create Fine** disabled rehta hai. Fine **Open** status mein save hota hai aur us rental par Fine charge lag jata hai. TRAX khud fine add, charge, waive ya collect nahi karta.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:fines:en -->
Open **Fines** from the sidebar to reach **Fines Management**. Search by reference, vehicle or customer, or filter by **Status**, **Issue Date** and **Due Date**; an open fine past its due date shows **Overdue**. Select the eye icon under **View** to open **Fine Details**, which has **Overview** and **Documents** tabs. For an **Open** fine, the row menu offers **Record Payment** and **Waive Fine** to staff with fines edit permission, and the detail page shows the same two buttons. **Record Payment** opens the payment dialog with the fine amount already set, and the fine status updates after the payment is recorded. **Waive Fine** acts immediately with no confirmation step: the fine becomes **Waived** and its charge is removed. With edit permission you can tick rows and use **Charge Selected**, **Waive Selected** or **Email Statement + Pay Link**; each asks for confirmation, and the email option needs unpaid fines for one customer and one rental with an email on file. TRAX does not look up, pay, charge or waive fines.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:fines:ur-Latn -->
Sidebar se **Fines** kholein, **Fines Management** screen aa jayegi. Reference, vehicle ya customer se search karein, ya **Status**, **Issue Date** aur **Due Date** se filter karein; due date guzarne wala open fine **Overdue** dikhata hai. **View** column mein aankh wale icon se **Fine Details** khulta hai, jis mein **Overview** aur **Documents** tabs hain. **Open** fine ke row menu mein fines edit permission wale staff ko **Record Payment** aur **Waive Fine** milte hain; detail page par bhi yehi do buttons hain. **Record Payment** payment dialog kholta hai jis mein fine ki amount pehle se set hoti hai, aur payment record hone ke baad fine ka status update ho jata hai. **Waive Fine** foran chal jata hai, koi confirmation nahi maangta: fine **Waived** ho jata hai aur us ka charge hat jata hai. Edit permission ho to rows tick karke **Charge Selected**, **Waive Selected** ya **Email Statement + Pay Link** use karein; har aik confirmation maangta hai, aur email option ke liye aik hi customer aur aik hi rental ke unpaid fines aur customer ki email zaroori hai. TRAX khud fines dhoondta, pay, charge ya waive nahi karta.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:invoices:en -->
Open **Invoices** from the sidebar. It has two tabs. **Invoices** lists rental invoices; they appear when rentals are created, and this page has no create button. Search by invoice number, customer or vehicle, filter by **Status**, set an **Invoice Date** range with **From** and **To**, and use **Clear Filters** to reset. The download icon exports the filtered list as a CSV file. Each row's menu offers **Send Email** and **Delete** to staff with invoices edit permission. **Send Email** opens **Send Invoice Email** with a preview, **Print / Save PDF** and **Send Email**; sending is disabled if the customer has no email address. **Delete** opens **Delete Invoice**; confirming with **Delete Invoice** removes the record permanently. The **Payment Requests** tab lists payment links and charges sent to customers with **Sent**, **Customer**, **For**, **Amount** and **Status**, and can be searched by customer or type. TRAX does not create, send or delete invoices and cannot confirm whether an invoice has been paid.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:invoices:ur-Latn -->
Sidebar se **Invoices** kholein. Is mein do tabs hain. **Invoices** tab mein rental invoices hoti hain; ye rental banne par khud aati hain, is page par invoice banane ka koi button nahi. Invoice number, customer ya vehicle se search karein, **Status** se filter karein, **From** aur **To** se **Invoice Date** range lagayein, aur **Clear Filters** se reset karein. Download icon filtered list ko CSV file mein export karta hai. Har row ke menu mein invoices edit permission wale staff ko **Send Email** aur **Delete** milte hain. **Send Email** se **Send Invoice Email** khulta hai jis mein preview, **Print / Save PDF** aur **Send Email** hain; customer ki email na ho to bhejna disabled rehta hai. **Delete** se **Delete Invoice** khulta hai; **Delete Invoice** dabane se record hamesha ke liye delete ho jata hai. **Payment Requests** tab mein customers ko bheje gaye payment links aur charges **Sent**, **Customer**, **For**, **Amount** aur **Status** ke saath dikhte hain, aur customer ya type se search ho sakta hai. TRAX invoice banata, bhejta ya delete nahi karta, aur ye confirm nahi kar sakta ke invoice pay hui ya nahi.

<!-- trax:expenses:en -->
Expense tracking is not offered in this portal. **Expenses** is not listed in the sidebar, and opening the Expenses page directly shows a not-found page, whatever your role. The sidebar's finance entries are **Payments**, **Invoices** and **Fines**; managers see only the ones they have been granted. TRAX cannot add, edit or delete expenses, and it cannot make the Expenses tracker available.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:expenses:ur-Latn -->
Is portal mein expense tracking available nahi hai. Sidebar mein **Expenses** nahi hai, aur Expenses page seedha kholne par not-found page aata hai, chahe aap ki role koi bhi ho. Sidebar mein finance ke liye **Payments**, **Invoices** aur **Fines** hain; managers ko sirf woh dikhte hain jin ki unhein ijazat mili ho. TRAX expenses add, edit ya delete nahi karta, aur Expenses tracker ko available bhi nahi kar sakta.

<!-- trax:credits:en -->
Credits are platform credit your business buys from Drive247 to pay for e-signatures, verifications and messages; they are not renter payments. Open **Billing** in the sidebar and scroll to **Credits**. **Live credits** shows what remains. Set **Amount to buy** with the minus and plus buttons or by typing (at least 5 credits), then select the buy button to open Stripe checkout; afterwards you return to the same screen. Expand the usage and settings panel for **Transaction History**, the **Usage History** chart with service and period filters, and **Auto-Refill Settings**: turn on **Enable Auto-Refill**, set **When balance drops below** and **Top up amount**, then select **Save Settings**. Auto-refill charges your saved payment method. If Billing shows **Choose your plan** or **Payment required**, the Credits section is not on that screen. TRAX does not buy credits, change auto-refill or check your credit balance.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:credits:ur-Latn -->
Credits woh platform credit hain jo aapka business Drive247 se khareedta hai; in se e-signatures, verifications aur messages ki payment hoti hai, ye renters ki payments nahi. Sidebar se **Billing** kholein aur neeche **Credits** tak jayein. **Live credits** baqi credits dikhata hai. **Amount to buy** minus/plus buttons ya type karke set karein (kam az kam 5 credits), phir buy button dabayein; Stripe checkout khulega aur payment ke baad aap isi screen par wapas aayenge. Usage aur settings wala panel kholein: wahan **Transaction History**, service aur period filters wala **Usage History** chart, aur **Auto-Refill Settings** hain. **Enable Auto-Refill** on karein, **When balance drops below** aur **Top up amount** set karein, phir **Save Settings** dabayein. Auto-refill aapka saved payment method charge karta hai. Agar Billing par **Choose your plan** ya **Payment required** dikhe to wahan Credits section nahi hota. TRAX credits nahi khareedta, auto-refill nahi badalta aur credit balance check nahi karta.

<!-- trax:billing_subscription:en -->
**Billing** is what your business pays Drive247, not payments from renters. Open **Billing** in the sidebar; every staff role can open it. **Plan Details** shows your plan, status, amount, current period and next payment or access end date. Under **Billing Methods**, select **Add payment method** or **Manage payment methods**, then **Add or replace card**; card details are entered on Stripe's secure page, where choosing a primary card or removing one is also handled. **Credits** covers credit bought from Drive247, and **Invoices & Receipts** lists past invoices. When e-signing is included with your plan there is no Credits section; **Next invoice** shows the date and each line of your next bill instead, such as **Platform subscription** and any premium integration. If your plan offers premium integrations, each carries a crown on the **Integrations** board; open one and select **Subscribe** to add its monthly price to your next Drive247 bill as its own line. If a payment failed, **Payment required** offers **Update payment method** and, when available, **Pay your pending invoice**. Without an active plan the page shows **Choose your plan**. To cancel, select **Contact support** under **Need to cancel?**, optionally add a reason and select **Send request**; nothing is cancelled and billing continues until the team contacts you. TRAX cannot see billing amounts, change plans or cards, or cancel a subscription.
<!-- /trax -->
<!-- trax:billing_subscription:ur-Latn -->
**Billing** wo hai jo aap ka business Drive247 ko pay karta hai, renters ki payments nahi. Sidebar mein **Billing** kholein; har staff role isay khol sakta hai. **Plan Details** mein plan, status, amount, current period aur agli payment ya access khatam hone ki date hai. **Billing Methods** mein **Add payment method** ya **Manage payment methods**, phir **Add or replace card** chunein; card details Stripe ke secure page par bharte hain, aur primary card chunna ya card hatana bhi wahin hota hai. **Credits** Drive247 se khareeda gaya credit hai, aur **Invoices & Receipts** mein purani invoices hain. Jab e-signing aap ke plan mein shamil ho to Credits section nahi hota; us ki jagah **Next invoice** agle bill ki date aur har line dikhata hai, jaise **Platform subscription** aur koi premium integration. Agar aap ke plan mein premium integrations hain to har ek par **Integrations** board mein crown hota hai; use khol kar **Subscribe** dabayein to us ki monthly price aap ke agle Drive247 bill mein alag line ban kar jud jati hai. Payment fail ho to **Payment required** par **Update payment method** aur, agar mojood ho, **Pay your pending invoice** milta hai. Active plan na ho to **Choose your plan** dikhta hai. Cancel ke liye **Need to cancel?** ke neeche **Contact support**, chahein to wajah likhein, phir **Send request**; kuch cancel nahi hota aur team ke rabta karne tak billing jari rehti hai. TRAX billing amount nahi dekhta, plan ya card nahi badalta aur subscription cancel nahi karta.
