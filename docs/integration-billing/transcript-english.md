## Notes

1. **Paying in the dialog.** The price goes on the bill the operator already pays, with the card already on file, and nothing is charged that day. So "pay" is a one-click confirm, not a card form. If Ghulam meant a card form like onboarding, that is a change to discuss.
2. **Testing the billing day.** Northwind has no live Drive247 plan in production today, and its billing mode is `test`. To try Subscribe for real, northwind needs a plan first: subscribe it in Stripe **test mode** with a test card (4242 4242 4242 4242), which costs nothing.
3. **Coming soon.** Inshur, Turo Sync and CheckMyDriver still have "coming soon" panels with nothing to connect. They show the crown and their price, but Subscribe says "Coming soon" until they launch. You cannot pay for something that does not work yet.
