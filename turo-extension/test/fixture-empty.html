Why book with Turo

Cars from trusted hosts, from £29 per day, with cover included on every booking and roadside assistance as standard throughout your trip.

Prices vary by city, vehicle and trip length.

How it works

Browse cars from local hosts in your area, compare what is available on your dates, and book the one you want. Most hosts respond within an hour and many offer delivery to a station or airport for an additional fee.

Collect the car at the agreed time and place. Your host will talk you through anything you need to know, and photographs are taken at the start and end of every trip so both sides have a record of the condition.

Return the car where you agreed, refuelled or recharged to the level you started with. Payment is handled entirely through Turo, so there is no cash to exchange and no deposit to chase afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own insurance? Cover is included with every booking, and you choose the level of protection that suits you when you check out.

Can I cancel? Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your trip starts. After that, the host's cancellation policy applies to your booking.

Who can book? Anyone with a valid driving licence who meets the age requirement for the vehicle and passes our standard eligibility check.

What if something goes wrong during the trip? Roadside assistance is included as standard, and support is available at any hour of the day.
